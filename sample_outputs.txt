QUESTION:
What is inflation?

GENERATED ANSWER:
Inflation is the increase in the price of goods and services over time, which reduces purchasing power and impacts economic stability.

QUESTION:
What is a mutual fund?

GENERATED ANSWER:
A mutual fund is an investment vehicle that pools money from multiple investors to invest in diversified financial assets such as stocks and bonds.

QUESTION:
What is a stock market crash?

GENERATED ANSWER:
A stock market crash refers to a sudden and significant decline in stock prices across the financial market, often caused by panic selling and economic uncertainty.
